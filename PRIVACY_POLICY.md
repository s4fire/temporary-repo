# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Miruro PreMiD Activity ("the Activity") handles information when you use it.

## 1. Overview

The Activity is a community-made PreMiD activity designed to display information about what you are watching on Miruro in Discord Rich Presence.

The Activity does not operate a separate server or maintain a user database.

## 2. Information the Activity Processes

When the Activity is running, it may read information available from the Miruro webpage, such as:

- The anime title currently being watched.
- The episode number.
- The episode title, when available.
- Anime artwork or other publicly available media information used for the Rich Presence.

This information is used to construct the Discord Rich Presence displayed by PreMiD.

## 3. Information We Store

The Activity itself does not intentionally collect or store:

- Your name.
- Your email address.
- Your Discord account credentials.
- Your passwords.
- Your IP address.
- Your browsing history.
- Your watch history in a separate database.
- Payment information.

The Activity does not have its own user accounts or analytics database.

## 4. Discord and PreMiD

The Activity operates through PreMiD and Discord. Information displayed through Discord Rich Presence may therefore be processed by those services according to their own policies.

The Activity does not control how PreMiD or Discord handles information after it is transmitted to those services.

## 5. Miruro

The Activity reads information from Miruro while you are using the website. Miruro is a separate service and is not operated by the Activity developer.

You should review Miruro's own privacy practices and terms for information about how Miruro itself handles your information.

## 6. Local Development Mode

When the Activity is run through PreMiD Activity Developer Mode and pmd dev, the activity communicates with the local PreMiD extension through the development environment.

The Windows Startup method described in this repository only automates starting that local development process. It does not create a separate remote server operated by the Activity developer.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Activity may interact with or rely on:

- PreMiD
- Discord
- Miruro

Each service has its own policies and terms. This Privacy Policy only describes the Activity itself.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Activity is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Since the Activity does not intentionally operate a user database or collect personal information, it does not knowingly maintain profiles of users.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Activity changes or when clarification is needed. Updates will be published in this repository.

## 10. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Activity, please use the repository's GitHub Issues section.

By using the Activity, you acknowledge that this Privacy Policy describes the Activity's handling of information.
